# BA仔皮肤生成记录

这两张最终皮肤由项目拥有者提供的头像作为身份参考生成。头像与成品仅用于这份定制礼物；公开仓库前应由拥有者确认是否保留这些素材。

## 女王花猫

- 文件：`src/assets/skins/queen-handdrawn.png`
- 最终提示词：

```text
Make a clean production-ready transparent PNG of BA仔's default queen skin. It must exactly retain the newest simple hand-drawn doodle aesthetic of the original avatar: thick imperfect black ink outline, flat limited colors, white chubby fluffy kitten with a charcoal patch covering its upper viewer-right head, huge simple black round eyes, paws with pads, whiskers, dark-tipped tail, small yellow crown with purple heart. IMPORTANT: output with genuine transparent alpha outside the cat. Do NOT draw a checkerboard pattern, grid, pale rectangle, paper texture, or any backdrop—the PNG must have no visible pixels outside the character silhouette, as it will be composited inside a transparent desktop application. Keep it flat 2D, cute and compact, not glossy/3D/realistic/plastic/anime. One cat, centered, no text, no frame, no watermark.
```

## 雪天女王

- 文件：`src/assets/skins/snow-handdrawn.png`
- 最终提示词：

```text
Use the ORIGINAL attached cat avatar and the newest simple hand-drawn BA仔 as the style reference; ignore the two glossy polished generated images. Make a winter snow-day skin for the exact same BA仔 character, in the original avatar's simple cute doodle language: thick imperfect black ink outline, flat creamy white and charcoal-black colors, huge round black eyes, dark patch over the upper viewer-right head, tiny round paws, black paw pads, whiskers, dark-tipped tail, chubby compact body. The character must look like the same cat, only dressed for winter. Add a simple pale icy-blue knitted scarf/capelet with white fluffy trim and snowflake doodles, plus the same small yellow outlined queen crown with a purple heart, now with one tiny blue snowflake ornament. Let it hug a small simple snowball; include at most four small blue snowflake doodles floating nearby. Flat 2D sticker art only: no 3D, glossy, photorealistic, plastic, rendered, airbrushed, anime, background scenery, text, watermark, or frame. Actual transparent alpha background only. One cat, no crane/claw machine, no wings, antennae, insect anatomy, human body, or fly-like silhouette.
```
